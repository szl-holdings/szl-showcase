Doctrine v11

Labels are typed, not cosmetic

Source, running software, and model evaluations are separate claims. A green test is not a deployment; a deployment is not a proof. The vocabulary below is enforced by CI across the estate.

MEASURED

Taken from live instrumentation with the method named, for example joules via NVML.

MODELED

Computed from a stated model or simulation. Never blended into a measured figure.

DECLARED

Asserted by a source but not independently measured.

UNAVAILABLE

Could not be measured. Shown as the gap it is, never estimated into a number.

LIVE

Observed on a public surface at a stated time. A dated observation, not a promise.

SIMULATED · HISTORICAL

Simulated results are not production results. Historical evidence stays historical when a newer source exists.

CONJECTURE 1The Λ aggregator is formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, and its uniqueness remains a labeled conjecture. The CI gate that rejects an unproven proof is red on purpose: that red is the honesty signal, not a failure to hide.
